How it works

Two simple ways to park, one shared set of records.

About availability

This system is fully software based. Parking status is updated when a driver or a parking operator records an action — booking, arrival (check-in) or departure (check out). There are no physical sensors, RFID tags, cameras or barriers, so the accuracy of the display depends on these records being kept up to date.

1. Book in Advance

  1. Choose a parking facility.
  2. Pick your arrival date, time, how long you will park, and your vehicle type.
  3. Tap a free space — or take the recommended one.
  4. Enter your vehicle registration and confirm. Your digital ticket is ready.

When you arrive, open My Bookings (or your ticket link) and check in. If you do not arrive, the space is automatically released 30 minutes after your scheduled arrival (the hold period can be changed per facility) so it is not blocked forever.

2. Quick Parking

  1. Scan the QR code at the entrance (or pick the facility on this site).
  2. See the live grid of spaces — green means free.
  3. Tap a free space, enter your vehicle registration and type.
  4. Confirm. Your slot and directions appear with your ticket code.

When you leave, use Check Out and enter your ticket code. The space immediately becomes free for the next driver.

What the colours mean

Vacant — free to book Reserved — held for a booking Occupied — a car is recorded here Maintenance — closed

How the recommendation works

When you ask for a space, the system first removes every space that is closed, incompatible with your vehicle, not accessible when you need it, or already taken for your time. It then ranks what is left by distance from the entrance, floor preference (lower floors score better) and vehicle compatibility, and suggests the best-scoring space. You can always pick a different free space yourself.
